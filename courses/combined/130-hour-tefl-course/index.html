How it works

TEFL abroad or online with our 130-hour Premier Online TEFL course. This course combines self-paced online training with an in-person classroom course that will give you a taste of what TEFL really involves.

The course comprises 100 hours of online content, allowing you the flexibility to study at your own pace with 24/7 access. On the classroom course, you’ll join other aspiring TEFL teachers at a location of your choosing for a fun-packed weekend where you’ll have the opportunity to develop and deliver your own TEFL lessons. 

Learn directly from teachers both online and in the classroom. All of our online and classroom tutors are highly qualified and experienced EFL teachers, ensuring an industry-leading learning experience.

The timeframe to complete this course is 5 months. On average, students take around 12 weeks to finish it, but as most of the course is self-paced you can go at your own speed. If life gets in the way and you need more time, we have a range of extension options available.

Session breakdown

30-hour Virtual Classroom TEFL course outline

The 30-hour Virtual Classroom TEFL Course consists of 24 hours of virtual classroom sessions (6x 4-hour sessions) plus six hours of pre-session tasks, including further reading and lesson plan preparation.

In addition to everything contained in the 20-hour course this extended course also features a session on teaching young learners, and an additional teaching practices session.

Sessions are available on Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays from 10am to 2pm GMT.

    Session 1Session 2Session 3Session 4Session 5Session 6

Session 1

During this session you'll cover a range of topics, from classroom management and warmers, to grammar, techniques to encourage students to speak, and more. This session also includes a foreign language lesson, which will really help you to understand what it’s like for language learners and better empathise with them.

Session 2

During the second session you’ll take what you learnt during the previous one to develop and deliver your own lesson. You’ll get feedback from your tutor and peers and observe other trainee teachers like yourself in action.

Session 3

Session 3 covers topics such as pronunciation, error correction, grammar, how to conduct a needs analysis and understand levels, as well as looking at various teaching skills.

Session 4

Teaching practices are a key part of any classroom TEFL course, and so during session 4 you’ll deliver your second lesson.

Session 5

This session focuses specifically on teaching young learners. You’ll cover strategies and ideas for conducting engaging lessons for this age group.

Session 6

This final session involves delivering your third, and final, lesson to your peers.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q. In what order should I complete the 130-hour Combined TEFL Course?

    A. We recommend completing the online modules in the order outlined. The weekday classroom course can be taken at the beginning, middle or end of your studies, depending on what suits you.

  • Q. When will I receive my certificate?

    A. On completion of all the modules included on the 130-hour Combined TEFL Course (both classroom and online) you will be issued with a TEFL Org certificate and a digital VQA Diploma. Along with your TEFL certificate, you will also receive a letter of reference from your classroom course tutor.

  • Q. Do I need any previous qualifications/experience to take this course?

    A. No previous qualifications or experience are required to take this course. All you need is fluency in English.

  • Q. How long does it take to complete this course?

    A. You’re given 7 months to complete this course. Most people finish it in around 12 weeks, but with full time study it's possible to complete it within 4 to 6 weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q. Is 130-hour certification enough?

    A. 120 hours is the industry standard and what most employers look for, so as long as your course consists of at least 120 hours you’ll be able to use it to teach abroad or online. Online qualifications from accredited providers (like us!) are recognised worldwide.

  • Q. How can I contact my online tutor?

    A. If you need help with any of the topics covered on the course or assignments, just send an email to your online tutor. You will find their contact information in the welcome email they will send you after you sign up.

  • Q. Can I talk to other students on the course?

    A. Yes, of course! You will have access to our online forum where you can chat with other students on the same course as you. You can also join our Facebook group where you can share resources and find information on teaching English abroad and online.

  • Q. Is there a payment plan for the course?

    A. You’ll need to pay the full balance before starting your course, but we do offer flexible payment options. If you’re based in the UK, you can spread the cost over 3–4 interest-free instalments with Clearpay. UK and some EU customers can also use Klarna at checkout, which offers a similar pay-in-instalments option. If you prefer, PayPal sometimes provides the option to split payments over 3 months, allowing you to get started right away. For combined or classroom/virtual courses, you can also reserve your place with a deposit if you're outside of the UK, with the balance due two weeks before your course date.

  • Q. How do I enrol?

    A. Enrolling is quick and easy:
    Online: Sign up at any time through our website; we accept all major credit and debit cards, plus PayPal.
    By phone: Call us and an adviser can enrol you directly.

    A. If you do find that you need more time to complete your TEFL course, we do offer course extensions. You can purchase a course extension at any time – even after your course has expired. These range from as little as one week up to three months in length.

    A. You will have access to a personal tutor who will mark and provide feedback on each of your written assignments. They will also be on hand to answer any questions you have about the topics covered in the course.

    A. All required learning materials are included in the course fee. We do recommend purchasing a good grammar reference book when you start teaching English, but this is not essential to complete the course. Browse grammar reference books on our Online Shop.

    A. Don’t worry, a detailed knowledge of English grammar isn’t essential before you start your TEFL training. All of our online course options include a module dedicated to English grammar and language, which covers everything you need to begin teaching it with confidence.
