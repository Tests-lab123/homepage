The benefits of classroom TEFL training

Bring TEFL to life with a classroom TEFL course. Get practical experience teaching English as a foreign language and connect with other aspiring TEFL teachers. Why should you include a classroom course as part of your TEFL training? There are lots of reasons, but here are three to start with:

  • Become a more confident teacher. The feedback from our students is unequivocal: a classroom TEFL course will leave you feeling more confident and prepared ahead of your first TEFL job.
  • Learn directly from someone who’s been there and done it. All of our classroom TEFL courses are led by highly experienced and qualified instructors, so you’ll be in safe and capable hands throughout your training.
  • Boost your CV. Employers love to see applications from teachers who’ve completed some practical training!

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q. What course should I do?

    A. We offer a range of courses to suit different schedules and needs. All of our 120+ hour courses will prepare you to start teaching English abroad or online for the first time. Take our quick quiz to find out what course is the best fit for you!

  • Q. Are TEFL Org courses accredited?

    A. Course providers as a whole are accredited, not individual courses. The TEFL Org is the most accredited TEFL course provider in the world, which means our qualifications are recognised by employers around the globe.

  • Q. How long does it take to get TEFL certified?

    A. With full-time study, it’s possible to complete one of our 120+ hour courses in as little as 4-6 weeks. Most of our students are studying part-time and the average completion time is 10-12 weeks.

  • Q. Can I use my qualification to teach English online?

    A. Yes! All of our 120+ hour courses are suitable for teaching both abroad and online. For those aiming to teach online, we recommend adding on our advanced course in Teaching English Online.

The world’s most accredited TEFL course provider

Accreditation matters. It's how both you and employers can be certain that a course offers all it claims to. That's why we are regularly assessed by a number of external bodies to ensure that the training we offer continues to be of industry-leading quality. These bodies include: DEAC, BAC, Ofqual, and SQA.

As the most accredited TEFL course provider in the world, a certificate from The TEFL Org is internationally recognised and respected.
