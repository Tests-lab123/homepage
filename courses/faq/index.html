Frequently Asked Questions

To get you started, we've listed some of the most popular questions we get asked about TEFL. If you have a question that has not been covered in our TEFL FAQs, please get in touch with our TEFL advisers who will be happy to help.

General TEFL / TESOL Questions

What is TEFL?

TEFL stands for Teaching English as a Foreign Language. It involves teaching English to non-native speakers, usually in countries where English isn’t the first language. With a TEFL qualification, you can teach abroad, online, or even in English-speaking countries.

Can non-native English speakers teach English?

Yes, non-native speakers can teach English. However, some employers may prefer native speakers, so job opportunities can be more competitive. A strong TEFL qualification and proof of English proficiency can greatly improve your chances.

Are there any age restrictions?

Our courses are open to anyone aged 18 and over. While there's no upper age limit, some countries may have age-related work visa requirements, so it’s worth checking local regulations before applying for jobs abroad.

I am a qualified teacher. Do I still need a TEFL certificate to teach English abroad?

Yes. Most schools abroad still require a TEFL certificate, even if you're a qualified teacher, as it provides specific training in teaching English as a foreign language.

Can I take a TEFL course if I have a disability?

Yes. We welcome learners with disabilities. If you need any specific support or adjustments, just get in touch - we’re here to help.

Are Groupon TEFL courses the same?

No, Groupon TEFL courses are often not widely recognised by employers and may come with hidden fees or lack essential support and accreditation.

The TEFL Org Certificates and Accreditation

Why The TEFL Org?

We’re the most accredited TEFL course provider, with certifications recognised worldwide. Our courses are developed by experienced EFL teachers and include dedicated tutor support and lifetime access to our TEFL Jobs Centre. While some courses may seem cheaper, our comprehensive training ensures you're well-prepared for your teaching journey.

Why is accreditation important?

TEFL accreditation means a course has been independently reviewed to ensure it meets high standards in content, delivery, and tutor support - giving you confidence in its quality and recognition.

What is TEFL accreditation?

TEFL accreditation means that a course provider has been thoroughly checked by independent bodies to ensure they deliver training and support that meets recognised quality standards. This includes reviewing course content, tutor qualifications, assessment methods, and student support. At The TEFL Org, both internal and external inspectors regularly check our materials and feedback to make sure they meet the highest academic standards.

Will a TEFL Org certificate get me a job?

A TEFL Org certificate is widely recognised and has helped thousands of graduates teach English online and around the world. You’ll get lifetime access to our TEFL Jobs Centre, plus CV support to help you stand out to employers. To help you apply with confidence, we provide guidance on writing your CV (résumé) so you can highlight your skills effectively and stand out to employers. You can explore our dedicated TEFL CV & résumé guide.

Is there an international TEFL accrediting body?

No, there isn’t one single global accrediting body for TEFL. Instead, look for courses with recognised external accreditation to ensure quality and credibility.

What is the difference between regulation and endorsement?

For standard courses like the 120-hour Online TEFL Course, your digital certificate is available immediately on your dashboard as soon as you pass. A hard copy is also available free of charge on request.

For Level 5 courses, once you pass your assessments, your work first goes through an internal quality check. When this is complete, your TEFL Org digital certificate will appear on your dashboard, usually within one week. You’ll also receive a digital certificate or diploma from our awarding body, VQA, which they will email directly to you. Before VQA can issue this, however, your work must undergo internal and external inspection process to make sure all tutor feedback matches the strict marking criteria. This ensures you have received the highest quality tutoring and assessment, and the process can take up to one month.

When will I receive my TEFL certificate?

You’ll get a PDF certificate within 24 hours of course completion. A hard copy is also available free of charge on request.

Does the TEFL certificate expire?

No, your TEFL certificate is valid for life. However, it's best to use it within two years of qualifying, as employers may ask about gaps in teaching experience.

What does it say on my certificate?

Your certificate includes your name, course title, completion date, and details such as the number of study hours.

Where can I get my TEFL certificate legalised?

Contact The TEFL Org for guidance on how to legalise, certify, or apostille your certificate. Requirements vary depending on the country where you plan to teach, and we can help you understand the process. You can also find more info about this on: Legalising Your TEFL Certificate.

I’ve lost my certificate. How do I order a new one?

If you’ve lost your certificate, simply contact our Customer Services team by emailing info@tefl.org to request a replacement copy. We’ll be happy to help. The cost is £10 for UK delivery or £15 for international delivery. If you’d prefer a tracked service, we can arrange delivery via DHL for £20. We’ll be happy to help you get a new copy quickly.

How can an employer verify my certificate?

Employers can verify your certificate through our official TEFL Certificate Verification page. They can also contact The TEFL Org directly, with your permission, to confirm the validity of your certificate.

TEFL Courses

Choosing & Entry Requirements

What qualifications do I need to apply for a course?

You don’t need any previous teaching experience or academic qualifications to get started. If English is your first language, you’re ready to apply. If it isn’t, we recommend that you have at least a C1 level of English (advanced) to ensure you can successfully complete the course and feel confident teaching.

What’s the difference between a classroom, virtual-classroom, and fully-online TEFL course?

Classroom courses: In-person training sessions held at certain locations. Virtual-classroom courses: Live, interactive sessions conducted online via platforms like Zoom. Fully-online courses: Self-paced study completed entirely online, accessible anytime.

How do I enrol?

Simply choose your preferred course on our website, add it to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Once your payment is confirmed, you'll receive immediate access to your course materials.

Pricing & Payments

Are there any hidden fees?

No, everything is included - course materials, tutor support, assessments, certification, and even postage for your certificate if you request a hard copy.

Can I pay in instalments?

You’ll need to pay the full balance before starting your course, but we do offer flexible payment options.

If you’re based in the UK, you can spread the cost over 3–4 interest-free instalments with Clearpay. UK and some EU customers can also use Klarna at checkout, which offers a similar pay-in-instalments option. If you prefer, PayPal sometimes provides the option to split payments over 3 months, allowing you to get started right away.

For combined or classroom/virtual courses, you can also reserve your place with a £50 deposit, with the balance due two weeks before your course date.

Klarna's Pay in 3 / Pay in 30 days are unregulated credit agreements. Borrowing more than you can afford or paying late may negatively impact your financial status and ability to obtain credit. 18+, UK residents only. Subject to status. Ts&Cs and late fees apply.

Tutor Support & Learning Experience

What support will I receive on the course?

You’ll get access to a personal tutor, comprehensive course materials, and our exclusive Discord community for students and graduates - so you're fully supported every step of the way. You’ll also be able to connect with other learners through our TEFL Org forum. With all these resources, you’ll be fully supported every step of the way.

How long does it take to receive feedback on assignments?

You’ll receive assignment feedback within 48 hours of submission.

How do I contact my online course tutor?

When you enrol, you’ll receive a welcome email with the name and contact details of your personal tutor. You can email your tutor directly using these details. You’ll also find your tutor’s contact information in the contact sheet within your course portal, so you can easily reach out whenever you need support.

What happens if I disagree with tutor feedback?

If you disagree with feedback, you can always request clarification or ask for a review of your assignment. To do this, simply email our support team at support@tefl.org, and we’ll be happy to look into it. We’re here to help you succeed.

Do tutors speak British or American English?

Our team of tutors includes both UK- and US-based teachers, so you’ll be supported by experienced professionals who are native speakers of either British English or American English. All tutors are highly qualified and will guide you through the course, whichever English variety you’re most familiar with.

My grammar isn’t the best. Will this be an issue?

No, grammar is covered in the course, and prior detailed knowledge is not required.

Do I need to purchase any textbooks for the course?

No, you don’t need to buy any textbooks to complete the course. All the materials you need are included and fully accessible through our online platform.

While not required, we do recommend considering a grammar reference book once you begin teaching. It can be a valuable resource for lesson planning or exploring grammar topics in more depth. However, rest assured that the course itself provides everything necessary for you to successfully train and qualify as a TEFL teacher.

Online TEFL Courses

Does the course prepare me for both online and in-person teaching?

Yes. Our courses are designed to give you the skills to teach in both classroom and online settings, with adaptable methods and practical strategies for each.

How long will it take to complete the online TEFL course?

Our online courses are self-paced. Depending on the program you choose, you’ll have 5–7 months to complete it, though most students finish in 10–12 weeks. You’re welcome to move faster or slower within your completion window.

What are the technical requirements for online TEFL courses?

To take an online TEFL course with The TEFL Org, you’ll need a PC or Mac running Windows 10 or later or iOS 10.2 or later. You can also access the course on a tablet or smartphone, although these devices may have limited functionality.

If you plan to attend a virtual classroom (VC) session, you’ll need to use a PC or Mac as tablets and smartphones don’t support all the required Zoom features.

All course materials are available directly through our online platform, but if you’d like to download resources, you’ll need Adobe Acrobat Reader.

In which order should I complete the course?

While our courses are flexible, we strongly recommend starting with Grammar followed by Methodology, as these modules lay the foundation for everything that comes after.

For the 120-hour course, the suggested order is:
• Grammar
• Methodology
• Video Observation
• Large Classes
• Remote Learning

For the Level 5 course, the suggested order is:
• Grammar
• Methodology
• Large Classes
• Remote Learning

Once you’ve completed Grammar and Methodology, the remaining modules can be taken in any order.

What if I need more time to complete my course?

You can purchase extensions ranging from one week to three months. Extensions can be arranged directly through your student dashboard, making it quick and easy to add the time you need.

Combined TEFL Courses (Online + Classroom)

What qualifications do I need to apply for a combined course?

You don’t need any prior teaching experience or qualifications. As long as you’re fluent in English, you’re good to go.

In which order should I complete the online and classroom parts?

It’s flexible! You can start with either. However, we recommend completing the online grammar section before attending the classroom session, as it will give you a solid foundation and boost your confidence.

Do TEFL Org combined courses include live teaching practice?

Yes. During the classroom part, you'll take part in teaching practice with feedback from your tutor and peers. This is a great way to build confidence.

Level 5 TEFL Diploma

Should I do a 120-hour or Level 5 TEFL course?

It depends on your career goals. A Level 5 course offers more in-depth training and may be preferred for competitive roles, but a 120-hour course is widely accepted and sufficient for many teaching jobs.

What is the benefit of a Level 5 TEFL course?

A Level 5 TEFL course offers more in-depth training, which can boost your confidence and make your job applications more competitive, especially for higher-paying or more selective roles.

Is this Level 5 Diploma internationally recognised?

Our Level 5 TEFL Diploma is regulated by Ofqual (UK government) and recognised by employers worldwide, ensuring your qualification is respected wherever you choose to teach.

What makes a course “Level 5”?

A Level 5 TEFL course is a qualification assessed at Level 5 on the UK’s Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF). Only courses regulated by an Ofqual-recognised awarding body (like VQA) meet this standard.

Advanced TEFL Courses

Do I need a core TEFL certificate before taking an advanced course?

Yes. Our advanced courses are designed to build on the foundational skills from a 120-hour TEFL certificate. You can enrol once you’ve completed your initial TEFL training.

Can I add on further TEFL courses later?

Yes – you can always build on your TEFL qualification by enrolling on additional courses at a later date. You could take one of several advanced courses to enhance your CV and increase job prospects. Learn more: Advanced TEFL courses

Are advanced courses accredited and internationally recognised?

Yes. As an accredited TEFL course provider, our advanced courses meet high standards and are widely recognised by employers around the world. Learn more about our accreditation here: The TEFL Org Accreditation

Can I bundle multiple advanced courses for a discount?

We offer TEFL course bundles that combine our foundational courses with advanced courses at a reduced price.

Classroom TEFL Courses (In-Person)

Where are classroom courses held?

Courses are offered in towns and cities across the UK, Ireland, and Spain.

How many people are there on the course?

There are typically 4 to 20 students per classroom.

What time does the course start and finish?

Weekday courses run 9 AM–6 PM, Monday to Wednesday. Weekend courses are 9 AM–8 PM on Saturday, and 9 AM–6 PM on Sunday.

Are there any breaks on the course?

Yes, there are short breaks, including a lunch break.

Is lunch included?

No, lunch is not included. You can bring your own or purchase it nearby.

Is accommodation provided?

No, accommodation is not included in the course fee.

Virtual Classroom TEFL Courses (Zoom Classes)

Is virtual teaching practice recognised by schools/employers?

Yes, virtual teaching practice is valued by employers, especially when in-person options aren't available. While some schools may prefer classroom experience, assessed online practice still shows you're prepared to teach.

How many people are there on the course?

There are typically 4 to 20 students per classroom.

When is the course held?

Our 20-hour Weekend TEFL course is made up of 4x4 hour sessions, with a range of time slots available. We also offer an Express option held over a single weekend. Our 30-hour Weekday TEFL course runs across 6 sessions, from 12pm - 4pm BST.

What technical set up is required to take part in the Virtual Classroom Course?

PC, Mac or laptop computer with a webcam and Zoom (the free version).

Audio Course

Course Overview

Is the audio course the same as the 120-hour Premier Online Course?

Yes – the audio course covers the same content as our 120-Hour Premier Online Course. The main difference is the learning format, giving you the flexibility to listen to lessons alongside a written transcript.

Is an audio version available for other courses, such as the Level 5 or advanced courses?

At the moment, the audio format is only available for the 120-Hour TEFL Course. We're exploring the possibility of rolling this out to additional courses in the future, but there are no confirmed plans to share at this time.

Is the audio course only available in British English?

At launch, the Audio TEFL Course is available in British English. We plan to introduce a US English version in the future, but we don't have a confirmed release date just yet.

Learning Experience

How does the audio course work?

The course combines audio lessons with interactive learning, allowing you to listen to course content on the go while still completing quizzes and assessments online at your own pace.

How long are the audio lessons?

Most audio lessons are designed to be short and manageable, typically between 10–30 minutes, making it easy to fit learning around your day.

Can I pause and resume lessons?

Yes – you can pause and resume lessons whenever you like, so you can learn at a pace that suits you.

Can I adjust the playback speed?

Yes – you can adjust the playback speed to make listening faster or slower depending on your learning style and preference.

Are transcripts included?

Yes – written transcripts are included alongside the audio lessons for added flexibility and accessibility.

How do I access the audio course?

You'll access the audio course the same way as other courses, via your student dashboard. From there, the 'Go to Course' button will take you to the dedicated audio course player rather than the standard course player.

How is my progress recorded?

Progress is recorded in the same way as on our standard courses. This also applies when listening via a lock screen or backgrounded app – lessons will be marked as complete as autoplay moves on to the next lesson.

Devices & Access

What devices can I use?

You can access the course on most modern devices, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.

Can I complete the course on my phone?

The course is designed to be mobile-friendly in all aspects, so you can complete it on any modern smartphone or tablet. For written assignments, however, you may find it more comfortable to use a PC or laptop if one is available.

Can I access the course on multiple devices?

Yes – you can log in and access your course across multiple compatible devices.

Do I need to download an app?

No – the course can be accessed directly through your web browser, so there's no need to download a separate app.

Is the course available offline?

Unfortunately, the course is not currently available for offline listening or download. You will need an internet connection to access the platform and complete assignments. We are exploring offline access options for future updates.

Assessments & Certification

Will my certificate show that I studied via audio?

No – your certificate is identical to the standard 120-hour course certificate and does not mention the learning format.

Will I receive the same certificate as the 120-hour Premier Online Course?

Yes – you'll receive the same internationally recognised TEFL certificate as learners completing the standard online course.

Will I still have access to tutor support?

Yes – you'll have access to a personal tutor throughout your course, just as with our 120-hour Premier Online Course.

Do I need to record myself speaking?

No – there is no voice recording or spoken interaction required in the audio course.

How do I complete quizzes and assignments?

Quizzes are completed within the learning modules as you go. Written assignments are submitted in the same way as on our standard courses.

Support & Technical Help

I've already paid for the 120-hour Premier Online Course. Can I switch to the Audio Course?

Unfortunately, we're unable to change course formats once you have started your course.

What should I do if my audio course stops working?

If playback stops unexpectedly, try refreshing your browser, checking your internet connection, or restarting the lesson. If the issue persists, our support team will be happy to help.

TEFL Jobs

Do I need a university degree to teach English abroad?

Not always. Some countries or schools require a degree for visa or hiring purposes, but there are many opportunities available for non-degree holders as well.

Do I need to speak the local language?

No, English is the only language used in the classroom. That said, learning a few local phrases can be helpful for daily life and cultural integration.

What are the best TEFL destinations?

The best TEFL destinations vary based on your goals—whether you're seeking high salaries, cultural immersion, or a relaxed lifestyle. Countries like South Korea, Vietnam, Japan, Spain, and Thailand are popular choices for English teachers abroad. Each offers unique opportunities and experiences. For a detailed overview, you can refer to this guide: Top TEFL Destinations This Year.

Can I teach English if I have a criminal record?

It depends on the nature of the offense and the visa requirements of the country where you want to teach. Some countries may restrict work visas based on certain criminal records.

Are you guaranteed a job after the course?

No job is guaranteed, but around 80% of our graduates find work within two months. You'll need to apply and interview, but we provide tools and support to help you succeed.

Can TEFL Org set me up with a job on completion of the course?

While we don’t place you in a job directly, you’ll get lifetime access to our TEFL Jobs Centre, where you can search and apply for teaching opportunities worldwide.

Will I be able to apply for a job straight after the course?

Yes, once you’ve completed your training, you’ll be fully equipped to apply for TEFL jobs and start teaching online or in the classroom.

Do I need teaching experience to find a good teaching position?

You don’t always need previous teaching experience to get started. Many entry-level TEFL jobs only require a recognised TEFL certificate, and there are plenty of opportunities for newly qualified teachers. Some positions, however, do ask for prior experience, especially more competitive or specialised roles.

I completed my TEFL course through a different course provider. Can I still apply for roles advertised on the TEFL Jobs Centre?

No, the TEFL Jobs Centre is exclusively for TEFL Org graduates. However, you can explore our Recommended TEFL Jobs listings or read our guide on Where to Find TEFL Jobs for other opportunities.

Teaching English Online

Which TEFL Org course is best if I plan to teach English exclusively online?

Our 120-hour Premier Online TEFL course is a great starting point for teaching English online. For more comprehensive training, consider our 250-hour Level 5 TEFL Diploma, which offers deeper insights into teaching methodologies.

Is the 120-hour certificate enough for online platforms, or should I take the Level 5 Diploma?

The 120-hour certificate meets the basic requirements for many online teaching platforms. However, the Level 5 Diploma provides more in-depth training, which can enhance your qualifications and open up more opportunities.

Can I teach English online without a university degree or native-speaker status?

Yes, many online platforms hire teachers without a university degree or native-speaker status. A TEFL certification and proficiency in English are often sufficient.

How do I find legitimate online-teaching jobs and avoid scams?

Use trusted job boards like TEFL Org’s Jobs Centre. Be cautious of platforms requiring upfront fees or offering unrealistic salaries. Research companies thoroughly before applying.

Teach English Locally

Can I use TEFL certification to teach in my home country?

Yes, many TEFL graduates use their qualification to teach English locally – either in schools, language centres, or through private tutoring. Just check if additional requirements apply in your country.
